# Code of Conduct

This project should be a respectful, practical place to collaborate.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind and direct.
- Assume good intent, especially across different backgrounds and experience levels.
- Keep feedback focused on the work, the code, and the user impact.
- Respect privacy. Do not ask people to share tokens, private bookmarks, exports, or account data.
- Help keep destructive workflow discussions careful and explicit.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Sexualized attention or unwelcome personal comments.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Repeated disruption after maintainers ask for a behavior to stop.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer. If a security issue is involved, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, block users, or take other reasonable action to protect the project and its contributors.

This code of conduct is intentionally short. The goal is not bureaucracy; the goal is a useful project where people can do good work without needless friction.
